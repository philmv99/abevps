What We Do

Critical Power & Backup

When the grid goes down — or before it even reaches the site — ABEV keeps power flowing: generators, transfer switches, battery storage, and jobsite power.

Downtime isn't an option. Design for it.

From standby generators that carry a building through an outage to temporary services that power a jobsite from day one, we build the systems that work when everything else doesn't — sized from real load data and commissioned properly.

Standby Generator Installation

Commercial and residential standby generators — electrical connections, generator feeders, equipment pads, site coordination, and startup.

Transfer Switches

Automatic and manual transfer switches installed and integrated cleanly with your distribution.

Battery & Solar Power

Battery backup systems and solar power connections for resilient, cleaner energy.

Temporary Power

Construction power, temporary panels and services, equipment power, and jobsite electrical distribution.

Commercial Equipment Connections

HVAC, motors, pumps, commercial kitchen, manufacturing, and specialty equipment — landed and energized.

Demolition & Relocation

Electrical demolition, equipment disconnects, circuit removal, EV charger removal, equipment relocation, and make-safe work.

Engineering

Sized right, installed right

A generator that's too small fails when you need it; too big wastes fuel and capital. We size from your actual loads, coordinate pads, fuel, and clearances, and commission the whole system — transfer switch to terminals.

Resilience

Beyond the generator

Resilience is going hybrid. Battery storage rides through short outages silently; solar connections cut the bill between them. Every install is rooted in the same mission as the rest of our work: cleaner power, done right.
